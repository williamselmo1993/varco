How it works

Three steps between "we should try AI agents" and agents safely running your back office.

01 · CONNECT
Plug in over MCP

Any MCP-compatible agent connects to Varco instead of your backend. Mock ERP included; REST and Odoo adapters built in.

02 · SET POLICY
Give each agent its lane

Read/write permissions per entity and autonomy thresholds per amount. In-policy actions run instantly — and get logged.

03 · APPROVE
Decide from your phone

Everything else becomes a plain-language card: review, fix values if needed, approve or reject with one tap.

What's in the box

The governance layer nobody ships: built for the approver, not just the developer.

Per-agent identity

Every agent is registered with its own permissions. Unknown agents are rejected at the first call.

Autonomy thresholds

Auto-approve under limits you set. No approval fatigue, no rubber-stamping — routine runs itself.

Review & edit

Approvers can correct the proposal before it executes. Not just yes/no.

Telegram approvals

One-tap approve/reject where you already are. WhatsApp Business next.

Immutable audit trail

Every read, request and decision recorded — your EU AI Act human-oversight evidence, built in.

ERP-aware semantics

Customers, orders, invoices — one vocabulary across mock, REST and Odoo backends. More adapters coming.
